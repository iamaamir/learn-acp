# 0003 — Demo is better, theme is dull

Learner on the Canvas lifecycle demo: "definitely a step up but surely not amazing".
On the course overall: "the whole theme in itself is very dull, a dull and less
fun interaction may take away the learning fun."

## Evidence
- Direct quotes; unprompted theme critique covering all three lessons.

## Challenges
- Visual delight is now the bottleneck, not conceptual difficulty or density.
- Risk: judging the Skoda by its paint — content is landing, packaging isn't.

## Engagement Pattern
- Learner cares about feel and play. Previously: wants animation over SVG steps.
  Now: wants a vivid theme + juicier demo (motion, glow, game-feel).

## Adjust
- Reskin globally: vivid but WCAG-safe palette (arcade-terminal direction),
  gradient header, colorful decision buttons, dark arcade Canvas stage.
- Juice the 0003 Canvas: easing, packet trails + glow, landing pops, pulsing
  traffic light, marching wire — all gated behind prefers-reduced-motion.
- Verify contrast ratios numerically before claiming "accessible fun".
- Do NOT rewrite lesson markdown/prose — same teacher, new paint.
